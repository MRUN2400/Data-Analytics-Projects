#  2.SuperStore Business Analysis Dashboard

A data analytics dashboard built using **Power BI** to analyze sales, profit, customer behavior, and category-wise performance of a retail SuperStore dataset. The dashboard helps identify business trends, profitable segments, low-performing categories, and customer purchasing patterns.
Project Overview

This project focuses on performing **business intelligence analysis** on retail sales data to extract actionable insights related to:

* Customer purchasing behavior
* Sales and profit trends
* Category-wise performance
* Geographic sales distribution
* Low-profit product identification
* Monthly sales and profitability trends

The goal is to help stakeholders make **data-driven business decisions** by visualizing important KPIs.

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

### 1. Profit by Category

Analyzes category-wise profitability across:

* Technology
* Office Supplies
* Furniture

**Insight:** Technology generates the highest profit.

---

### 2. Sales by Category

Compares total revenue generated by each product category.

**Insight:** Technology and Furniture contribute the highest sales.

---

### 3. Average Sales by Category

Tracks average category performance and sales contribution.

Used to identify:

* High performing categories
* Declining categories

---

### 4. Monthly Sales and Profit Analysis

Visualizes monthly business performance trends.

Helps analyze:

* Seasonal demand
* Revenue fluctuations
* Profit consistency over time

---

### 5. Sales by City

Shows geographic distribution of sales performance across cities.

Helps identify:

* High revenue cities
* Regional market opportunities

---

### 6. Low Profit Categories

Highlights categories generating lower profits despite sales volume.

**Insight:** Furniture contributes relatively lower profitability.
